# Red Sea Horizons
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Approved handoff: Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, next-intl, PostgreSQL, Supabase Auth/Storage and hosted provider checkout.

## Users
Travelers exploring the Egyptian Red Sea and requesting tours, diving, transport, photography or agency-managed Port Ghalib apartments. Guests use mobile devices, ten languages and guest checkout. Staff review reservations, manage capacity and fulfill approved trips from a desktop or mobile operations interface.

## Product purpose
Let a guest discover an offer, receive an authoritative itemized quote and submit a reservation request. Every request requires staff approval. No money is requested before approval.

## Operating context
Schedules use Africa/Cairo, stays use local dates and exclusive checkout. Staff operate scoped queues, shared departures, physical apartment inventory, financial obligations and private guest galleries.

## Capabilities and constraints
All ten approved locales: en, de, pl, fr, ru, uk, es, ar, it, nl. Arabic is RTL; phone numbers and references remain LTR. Guest grants use POST redemption, private pages remain uncached and noindex. Payment policies are full, deposit or arrival, with server-derived amounts. No fake reviews, business claims, social addresses or public source media.

## Brand and assets
The owner confirmed Red Sea Horizons and supplied a navy/turquoise/sand brand board. Supplied underwater photographs are local reference material; their publication requires consent evidence. Apartment imagery is explicitly illustrative until real unit images are approved.

## Decisions still open
